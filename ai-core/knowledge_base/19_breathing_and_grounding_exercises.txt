TOPIC: Breathing and grounding exercises for anxious moments
When anxiety spikes, simple exercises can calm the body within a few minutes. For slow breathing, sit comfortably, breathe in gently through the nose for about four counts, and breathe out slowly through the mouth for about six counts. Repeat for two to five minutes, letting the shoulders drop. For the 5-4-3-2-1 grounding exercise, name five things you can see, four you can touch, three you can hear, two you can smell and one you can taste. This brings attention back to the present. Another option is to press your feet into the floor and notice the weight of your body in the chair. These do not remove the cause of worry, but they can turn the volume down so you can think and rest. If you feel dizzy while breathing slowly, return to normal breathing. Persistent panic is worth mentioning to your doctor.
