TOPIC: Life after treatment ends
Finishing treatment is often expected to feel like a celebration, yet many people feel anxious, tired or lost when regular visits and the sense of active treatment stop. This is common and does not mean something is wrong. Energy often returns slowly over months, and a new normal may include different routines, scars, changes in body and mood, and worry before each follow-up. Follow-up appointments and scans check on your recovery and catch any problem early, and it is natural to feel nervous around them. Gentle regular activity, balanced meals, sleep and staying connected with people help recovery. Setting small goals, asking your team for a survivorship care plan, and joining a peer group can help. If low mood, fear or exhaustion keep going and affect daily life, talk to your doctor or a counsellor.
