TOPIC: Questions to ask your doctor
Appointments are short and emotions run high, so many people leave forgetting what they wanted to ask. It helps to write questions down beforehand, bring a family member or friend to listen and take notes, and ask if you may record the conversation. Useful questions include: What type and stage is my cancer, and what does that mean? What are my treatment options, and what do you recommend and why? What are the likely benefits and side effects? How will we know whether the treatment is working? What should I do if I feel unwell at home, and whom do I call? Is there anything I can do to help myself, such as food or activity? It is fine to say you did not understand and ask for it to be explained again in simpler words. You can also ask for a second opinion.
